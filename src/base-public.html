West Lake: bridges, causeways, an island and a pagoda

🗺️ Illustrated West Lake map and the lake in 2 minutes

Tap the picture to open it full size. It is a drawing, not to scale: for walking use the day map below (“All of West Lake” tab).

Why it matters. West Lake is the lake Chinese poets, painters and garden designers copied for a thousand years; it became a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2011. The two causeways are named after poet-governors who dredged the lake: Bai Juyi (Tang, 9th c.) and Su Dongpo (Song, 1089), who piled the mud into the Su Causeway. Dongpo pork is named after him too.

The White Snake legend. A snake spirit becomes the woman Bai Suzhen and meets the scholar Xu Xian at the Broken Bridge in the rain, when he lends her his umbrella. The monk Fahai later imprisons her under Leifeng Pagoda. You see both ends of the story today: the bridge in the morning, the pagoda in the evening.

The Ten Scenes of West Lake (西湖十景) you pass today:

  • ✓ Lingering Snow on the Broken Bridge (断桥残雪)
  • ✓ Autumn Moon over the Calm Lake (平湖秋月), east tip of Gushan
  • ✓ Three Pools Mirroring the Moon (三潭印月)
  • ✓ Viewing Fish at Flower Harbor (花港观鱼)
  • ✓ Spring Dawn at Su Causeway (苏堤春晓)
  • ✓ Leifeng Pagoda in Evening Glow (雷峰夕照)
  • ○ Evening Bell at Nanping Hill (南屏晚钟): Jingci Temple, opposite Leifeng
  • ○ Orioles Singing in the Willows (柳浪闻莺): east shore, on the way to Hubin
  • ○ Lotus in the Breeze at Quyuan (曲院风荷): north end of the Su Causeway
  • ○ Twin Peaks Piercing the Clouds (双峰插云): hills to the west

Costs for the two of you. Boat with island landing ¥110–140 (S$21–27). Leifeng ¥80 (S$15). Causeways, parks, Gushan and the fountain are free. Lunch at Louwailou about ¥250–350 (S$48–67).

If you are running late, keep in this order: Broken Bridge and Bai Causeway → boat to the island → Leifeng before 17:00. Drop first: the Xiling climb, then the Su Causeway walk.

Easy on the feet. Walking is about 6–8 km with everything. To cut it to about 4 km: Didi to the Broken Bridge in the morning, walk only the short Su Causeway section from Huagang, and Didi from Changqiao Park to Hubin in the evening.

Metro

Every ride

  1. Plan in Amap before going underground: line, direction (named after the last station), exit number.
  2. Bags through the X-ray at the entrance.
  3. Alipay → 乘车码 (ride code) → pick the city → hold the QR on the scanner. Scan in and out. In Shanghai you can also tap a contactless Visa or Mastercard.
  4. Check the platform screen for the direction. Transfers: follow 换乘 and the line colour.
  5. Leave by the exit number Amap shows (出口 = exit). Rides cost 2–5 RMB (S$0.4–1).
  6. With suitcases or in rush hour (07:30–09:30, 17:30–19:00): take a Didi instead.

Shanghai 上海

  • Hotel station: Dashijie (Line 8), 1 min. People’s Square (Lines 1, 2, 8) 10 min walk.
  • Bund: Line 2 → East Nanjing Road. Shanghai Tower: Line 2 → Lujiazui, exit 6.
  • Yu Garden: Line 8 → Laoximen, Line 10 → Yuyuan Garden, exit 1.
  • Wukang Mansion: Line 10 → Jiaotong University. Jing’an Temple: Line 2 or 7.

Suzhou 苏州

  • Garden and museum: Line 6 → 拙政园苏博.
  • From Pan Pacific, Didi is simpler: 15–20 min, about 15–25 RMB (S$3–5). Alipay code only at the gates.

Hangzhou 杭州

  • Hotel station: Ding’an Road (Line 1). Hangzhou East: Line 1 direct, about 25 min.
  • Grand Canal: Line 5 → Gongchen Bridge East. Xixi: Line 3 → Xixi Wetland South.
  • No metro to Lingyin, Longjing or Leifeng: Didi, bus or bike. Alipay code only at the gates.

Shared bikes

  • Alipay → search 哈啰 (Hellobike) → scan the bike’s QR. About 1.5 RMB (S$0.3) per 15 min.
  • Park only in marked zones (fines of 5–20 RMB (S$1–4)). Hangzhou’s red public bikes: first hour free.
